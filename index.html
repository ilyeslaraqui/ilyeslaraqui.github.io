How I work

The same loop I've used on wind tunnel parts, suspension, simulation models and a trading strategy.

  1. Define the problem
  2. Understand how things work today
  3. Design options
  4. Agree a direction with the team
  5. Simulate and test
  6. Correlate with real data
  7. Peer review
  8. Implement at the right moment
  9. Monitor, then close

About

I'm French and Moroccan and grew up moving every four years or so: London, Fes, Zurich, Cairo, then back to London. Changing countries that often taught me to settle into new teams quickly and to work across languages.

My interest in engineering started in a sports car garage in Alsace, where I rebuilt a Porsche 911 engine at 14. Since then I've led a 10+ person Formula Student team as Technical Director, mentored students in UCL's workshop, and judged F1 in Schools.

Outside work: motorsport strategy and car physics, Rubik's cubes (42 s best), football, basketball and handball, and karate (blue belt).
