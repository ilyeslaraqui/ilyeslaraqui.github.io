Nonlinear FEA against a tensile test

I processed tensile-test data for an aluminium specimen in MATLAB (Young's modulus 63.9 GPa, yield stress 116 MPa), then built an elastic-plastic model with ductile damage in Abaqus and ran a mesh-convergence study to pick the mesh. My predicted 54.7° shear-fracture angle matched both the FEA and lab footage of the real specimen breaking.

Control and vibration in MATLAB

I designed a PD controller that cut overshoot by about 21% and response time by about 80% compared with proportional control. I also built a six-mass vibration model, solved it in MATLAB and designed mounts that hit a 10.2% response reduction at 23 Hz within mass and stiffness limits.
