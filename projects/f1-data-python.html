Exploring F1 timing data in Python

  • Data & analysis

In my own time I use the FastF1 library to pull public Formula 1 telemetry.

Role
Personal project
When
2024 – present
Tools
Python, FastF1, matplotlib

Two of the scripts I keep coming back to overlay the speed traces of the same driver's fastest qualifying laps at one track in two seasons, with the corners marked, and colour a lap's racing line by the gear in use. Together they show where a car has gained from one year to the next and how differently drivers use the gearbox around a lap.
