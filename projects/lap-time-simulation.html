I set up 78 configurations, varying powertrain, aero package, differential, suspension geometry, toe and camber. I scored each one across Acceleration, Skidpad and Sprint and ranked the 63 that completed.

The ranking also showed which choices mattered. A single motor without the aero package scored highest, and toe and camber separated the top runs. A locked differential was the clear worst. In the following year's event, with a new track and car options, we placed 23rd of 57 teams.
