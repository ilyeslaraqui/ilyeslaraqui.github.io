Wheel assembly and tyre deformers

Tyre shape has a big effect on diffuser and rear brake duct flow, so the tyre in the tunnel has to match the shape it has on track. Starting from the aero surfaces, I designed the rims, hubs, wheel covers and the deformers that push the outboard shoulder of the tyre out to its loaded shape. I used one representative profile and revolved it through 360°.

I owned the parts end-to-end: released them to manufacturing, assembled and balanced the rims, and fitted the wheels to the model myself. The built assembly matched the FEA model to within 0.8 mm on a laser scan.

Radiator correlation rig

Model radiators are tested on a rig and correlated with CFD before they go on the tunnel model. I designed a full-size version of that rig concept: a 6 m "mini wind tunnel" built from 14 bonded and sanded sections, with a real radiator core mounted inside it as a cross-section.

It measures the pressure and velocity loss across the core, so the model radiator can be correlated against the real one.

A 5-year assembly problem, fixed

One model assembly had been unreliable for more than five years. I traced it to clearances tighter than 0.010 mm that no drawing captured, added the correct GD&T annotation, and checked the fix over 15 assembly iterations. All 15 assembled correctly.

Overnight fix for a part clash

At 20:00 I was the last mechanical engineer on site when the Model Shop found a part clash that had to be fixed before a 06:00 tunnel run. I up-issued the part, issued the new drawing within 3 hours, and stayed until it was made and fitted to the model for checking. The aero component it carried was then tested and released quickly as a track upgrade.

Rigs, model parts and race weekends

I designed several rigs for testing new technologies for the model, including a calibration rig and bench-test rigs, plus suspension pick-up parts checked against kinematic points. I also improved the model's metrology datum system. On race weekends I supported the race engineering team in the ops room by monitoring competitor radio.
